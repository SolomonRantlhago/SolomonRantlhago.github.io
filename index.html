About Me

I am a software development professional from Ga-Rankuwa, Pretoria, with a BSc in Information Technology from the University of the Free State, majoring in Computer Science and Business Management, and a National Certificate in IT (System Development), NQF Level 5, completed through the Umuzi learnership programme.

My professional experience includes working as a Junior Software Engineer at MIP Holdings, where I worked with Progress OpenEdge ABL and JasperReports/Jaspersoft Studio to support the development and maintenance of software solutions, including medical scheme communications and reporting-related tasks.

Alongside my professional experience, I have continued to strengthen my software development skills through practical learning and projects involving JavaScript, React, Python, Django, SQL, REST APIs, and database development. My academic background in programming, data structures, and database management, together with my industry experience and ongoing technical development, has helped me build a solid foundation for developing and maintaining software applications.

I am passionate about problem-solving, continuous learning, and building reliable, user-focused software solutions. My goal is to continue growing as a software developer, deepen my technical expertise, and contribute to projects that address real-world problems.

Projects

Snakes and Ladders Windows Forms Application

This project is a Windows Form application that emulates the classic Snakes and Ladders board game. Players interact with a dynamic and visually appealing interface, taking turns to roll virtual dice and navigate their tokens across a 36-block game board. The application features distinct classes for Board, Token, and Player, each designed according to specific interfaces. Users can input player names, roll dice, and observe token movements, with special events triggering informative messages on the interface.

GitHub Repository for the Snake and Ladders Game Application

Sesotho-English Translation Console App

This is a user-friendly console application designed for translating Sesotho words to English and vice versa. Leveraging a dataset of the 1000 most common Sesotho words and their English translations, the app allows users to display words alphabetically in both languages and perform quick lookups, offering a seamless language exploration experience
