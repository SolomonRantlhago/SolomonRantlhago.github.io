About Me

My name is Solomon Rantlhago, and I was born and raised in the vibrant community of Ga-Rankuwa, Pretoria. Growing up in Ga-Rankuwa instilled in me a profound appreciation for the significance of preserving our cultural identity. I nurtured my passion for problem-solving and creativity in an environment that values tight-knit support and community bonds. This love for finding solutions began during my high school years, particularly in the subjects of Science and Mathematics, and gradually expanded into the broader world of technology, setting the stage for my academic journey.

I completed both my primary and secondary education in Ga-Rankuwa. I discovered my passion for learning and built a strong foundation of knowledge during this time. This foundation motivated me to pursue higher education at the University of the Free State, where I earned a degree in BSc Information Technology, majoring in Computer Science and Business Management. Throughout my academic journey, I delved deeper into my love for problem-solving, particularly in data structures, advanced programming, and database management. I excelled in these subjects, earning distinctions. As I continued to refine my skills, I became increasingly interested in web development. I'm excited by the opportunities web development provides, from creating user-friendly websites to shaping the evolving digital landscape. My passion for technology and natural talent for finding solutions make me well-suited for this field, and I look forward to pursuing a career in web development.

Apart from my academic pursuits, I have developed a range of interests and hobbies that are an integral part of my life. I cherish playing soccer, but I'm also passionate about exploring technology and entrepreneurship through my reading. The ever-evolving world of technology and its association with entrepreneurship fascinates me. My reading habit has fueled my desire to make a difference in this field.

Projects

Snakes and Ladders Windows Forms Application

This project is a Windows Form application that emulates the classic Snakes and Ladders board game. Players interact with a dynamic and visually appealing interface, taking turns to roll virtual dice and navigate their tokens across a 36-block game board. The application features distinct classes for Board, Token, and Player, each designed according to specific interfaces. Users can input player names, roll dice, and observe token movements, with special events triggering informative messages on the interface.

GitHub Repository for the Snake and Ladders Game Application

Sesotho-English Translation Console App

This is a user-friendly console application designed for translating Sesotho words to English and vice versa. Leveraging a dataset of the 1000 most common Sesotho words and their English translations, the app allows users to display words alphabetically in both languages and perform quick lookups, offering a seamless language exploration experience
